What it is

A small, self-hosted bug tracker for beta-testing programs: testers report bugs with the device, software build and mode they were using, attach screenshots and logs, discuss them in comments, and follow each bug from Open to Fixed in build. Everything is stored in a single SQLite database on your own server.
